Add subscriptions: a customer subscribes to a plan and is invoiced every month.

- **Plans.** `POST /plans` takes `{"name": ..., "monthly_cents": ...}` (a whole number of cents above 0) and answers 201 with `{"id", "name", "monthly_cents"}`. `GET /plans/{id}` reads one.
- **Subscribing.** `POST /subscriptions` takes `{"customer_id", "plan_id", "start_date"}` and answers 201 with the subscription: `{"id", "customer_id", "plan_id", "status": "active", "anchor_day", "next_billing_date"}`. `start_date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) is optional and defaults to the tenant's today; a date in the past or not a real date is a 400. `anchor_day` is the start date's day of the month; the first billing date is the start date. `GET /subscriptions/{id}` reads one.
- **Billing dates and periods.** A subscription is billed on its anchor day every month. In a month that has no such day, it is billed on that month's last day, and goes back to the anchor day in the next month that has it (an anchor of 31: Jan 31, Feb 28, Mar 31, Apr 30). A period runs from a billing date to the day before the next billing date.
- **Running billing.** `POST /subscriptions/run-billing` is called by a daily job. For every active subscription of the tenant whose next billing date is on or before the tenant's today, it bills each period not billed yet, oldest first: one invoice per period, for the customer, with one line `"<plan name> <period start> to <period end>"`, quantity 1, at the plan's monthly price, and issues it. It answers 200 with `{"invoices": [the ids of the invoices it created]}`. Running it again on the same day creates nothing.
- **Changing plan.** `POST /subscriptions/{id}/change-plan` takes `{"plan_id"}`, answers 200 with the subscription, and takes effect on the tenant's today, for the rest of the current period and every later one. When the new plan costs more, it creates and issues an invoice at once with one line `"Upgrade to <new plan name> <today> to <period end>"`, quantity 1, at (new price minus old price) times the days left in the period, today included, divided by the days in the period. A change to a plan that costs the same or less creates no invoice. A subscription with a period that is due and not billed yet cannot change plan (409 conflict); a subscription that has not started yet changes plan with no invoice.
- **Canceling.** `POST /subscriptions/{id}/cancel` sets the status to `canceled` and answers 200 with the subscription; it is never billed again. Canceling a canceled subscription is a 409 conflict.
